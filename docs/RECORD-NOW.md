# Record the video

You read out loud. The app drives itself. You never touch the keyboard except
the space bar.

Total take: **4 minutes 15 seconds.** The limit is 5 minutes.

---

## Before you press record

**1. Open the app window.**

    https://b5emjsgbi1.execute-api.eu-north-1.amazonaws.com

Put it where you are going to record. Make the window big. This is the only
thing that appears in the video.

**2. Open the director in a SECOND window.**

    https://b5emjsgbi1.execute-api.eu-north-1.amazonaws.com/director

Put it on your other screen, or on your phone, or anywhere outside the area you
are capturing. **This window must never be visible in the recording.**

**3. Check the top-left of the director says `APP CONNECTED` in green.**

Either order works, and reloading the app window is fine — the director notices
and reconnects within about two seconds. If it says **`app lost`**, reload the
app window and it will pick it up again.

**4. Start your screen recording.** Capture only the app window.

**5. Press the space bar.**

---

## While recording

- The big white line is **what you say**. Read it as written.
- The orange line above it is **what is happening on screen** — for your eyes
  only, do not read it out.
- The grey line underneath is **what comes next**, so you can see the turn coming.
- The clock on the right says how long until the next line. When it reaches
  zero the director moves on by itself.

You do not need to click anything. The app clicks its own buttons.

**If you fumble a line:** keep going. Do not stop. One clean run with a stumble
beats four takes.

**If you want to stop:** press **P** to pause, breathe, press **P** again.

**If you need to go back one line:** press **←**.

---

## The two beats where the app is thinking

Two lines wait for real network calls, and the director will not move on until
the screen has caught up with your voice:

- **"Read the drawer…"** — about five seconds while it calls the NIH and the FDA.
  Keep talking; the line is written long enough to cover it.
- **"…I ask it to write the question."** — about three seconds on Bedrock.

The director shows `waiting for the app…` while this happens. That is normal.

---

## If something goes wrong mid-take

Stop recording. Press **R** on the director. That resets the app to a clean
drawer and puts you back at the start. Then record again.

Nothing needs restarting on the server side.

## After recording

1. Upload to YouTube. **Set it to Public or Unlisted** — a private video counts
   as no video.
2. Paste the link into the Devpost **Video demo link** field, which is the last
   empty required field.
3. Press Submit.
